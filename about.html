About Me

Hi, I'm Sheth Dhaivat Chandreshkumar, a passionate and dedicated student of Computer Engineering based in Bhavnagar, Gujarat. My journey in education began at SPEI School, Bhavnagar, where I completed my 10th grade. I then pursued a Diploma in Information Technology from BPTI, Bhavnagar, which laid the foundation for my interest in the tech world.

Currently, I’m advancing my skills by studying Computer Engineering at GMIT, Bhavnagar, affiliated with Gujarat Technological University (GTU). Alongside my academic journey in technology, I have also nurtured my love for Indian classical music and proudly hold a Vishaarad degree in Tabla.

With a strong mix of technical expertise and artistic passion, I aim to grow as a well-rounded individual and contribute meaningfully to both fields.
